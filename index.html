Integrated Digital Onboarding & Payments

One platform to identify, verify, link, and fund.

CoreBridge lets a credit union onboard a customer, verify their identity, securely link an external bank account, capture authorization, and move funds in — by ACH or FedNow — straight into the core. A single foundation that scales to new products, including Pay Express Money Transfer.
