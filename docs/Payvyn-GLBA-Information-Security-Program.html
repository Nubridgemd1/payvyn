Information Security ProgramGLBA Safeguards Rule (16 CFR Part 314) · Written (WISP)
Payvyn (BizFormCorp LLC) handles nonpublic personal information (NPI) as a service provider to financial institutions. This Written Information Security Program (WISP) implements the GLBA Safeguards Rule (16 CFR Part 314) and is the document partner institutions request in due diligence. Owner: [Qualified Individual]. Effective: [date]. Review: annual.

1Purpose & scope

To protect the security, confidentiality, and integrity of customer NPI that Payvyn receives, processes, or transmits on behalf of financial-institution clients. Scope: the Payvyn onboarding application, verification backend, data stores, supporting cloud infrastructure, and all personnel and service providers with access to NPI.

NPI handled: name, date of birth, SSN/ITIN, address, contact details, bank routing/account numbers, identity documents, and verification results.

2Qualified Individual

[Name / title] is designated as the Qualified Individual responsible for overseeing, implementing, and enforcing this program, and reports on it at least annually to BizFormCorp management / the Board.

3Risk assessment

  • Written risk assessment performed at least annually and on material change, covering confidentiality, integrity, and availability of NPI, and internal & external threats.
  • Risks are rated and mapped to the safeguards in Section 4; residual risk is reviewed by the Qualified Individual.

4Safeguards

Control areaImplementation
Access control & least privilegeRole-based access; access limited to those who need it; periodic access reviews; prompt deprovisioning.
Multi-factor authenticationMFA required for administrative and remote access to systems holding NPI.
EncryptionNPI encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-GCM). Full SSNs and account numbers stored server-side only — never persisted in the browser.
Data inventory & classificationDocumented inventory of NPI, where it is stored/processed, and its classification; minimization — collect/retain only what is needed.
Secure developmentSecure SDLC, code review, dependency/version pinning, secrets held in a managed secret store (not in code).
Change managementReviewed, logged deployments; ability to roll back; separation of duties where feasible.
Logging & monitoringSecurity-relevant events logged; anomaly/alerting; verification attempt-limiting to deter abuse.
Secure disposal & retentionNPI retained only as long as needed; secure deletion; retention aligned to FI/legal requirements (see Appendix).
InfrastructureHosted on reputable cloud providers with physical/environmental controls; data-handling governed by provider agreements.

5Service-provider oversight

  • Due diligence on providers that store or process NPI (e.g., cloud/infrastructure); written agreements requiring appropriate safeguards; periodic review of their security posture (e.g., SOC reports).
  • [Maintain a current list of subprocessors and their security attestations.]

6Incident response

  • Written incident-response plan: detect, triage, contain, eradicate, recover, and document.
  • Defined roles, escalation, and notification to affected financial-institution clients and, where required, regulators/consumers, within the timelines in client agreements and applicable law.
  • Post-incident review feeds the risk assessment and program updates.

7Training & personnel

  • Security-awareness training at onboarding and at least annually; role-specific training for engineers handling NPI.
  • Background checks and confidentiality agreements for personnel with NPI access [confirm].

8Monitoring & testing

  • Continuous monitoring of systems; regular vulnerability scanning; annual penetration test by a qualified third party [schedule].
  • Findings tracked to remediation with owners and due dates.

9Program governance

  • The Qualified Individual reviews and updates this program at least annually and upon material change, and reports in writing to management/Board (program status, risks, incidents, test results).
  • This WISP is provided to FI clients on request and incorporated into client data-security addenda.
