NACHA TPSP ComplianceThird-Party Service Provider role & annual Rules-compliance posture
This document states Payvyn's role under the Nacha Operating Rules, the obligations it meets, and the annual Rules-compliance posture that supports the ODFI and the Third-Party Sender.
Payvyn is a Third-Party Service Provider (TPSP) — not a Third-Party Sender (TPS).
Payvyn performs ACH functions (account verification, NACHA file creation) but does not hold the origination agreement with the ODFI and does not transmit entries into the ACH network. The Originator / TPS (e.g., BizFormCorp / PayExpress) holds the ODFI agreement, is registered with Nacha, and completes the annual Rules Compliance Audit; Payvyn supports and enables that compliance.

1Roles & responsibilities

PartyResponsibility
ODFI (credit union/bank)Originates entries into the ACH network; warrants entries; registers the TPS with Nacha; sets exposure limits; conducts due diligence.
TPS / Originator (BizFormCorp)Holds the origination agreement with the ODFI; obtains authorizations; manages return rates; completes the annual Rules Compliance Audit (Nacha Appendix Eight) and risk assessment; registered with Nacha.
Payvyn (TPSP)Prepares NACHA files, verifies accounts, and supports authorization/return handling — under the direction of the Originator/ODFI. Does not originate or hold the agreement.

Returns & NOCsProcesses returns (R-codes) and NOCs by trace/reference; supports the Originator in keeping return rates below thresholds (unauthorized 0.5%, administrative 3%, overall 15%).

3Annual Rules-compliance posture

  • Supports the Originator's annual Rules Compliance Audit (Nacha Appendix Eight) by providing evidence of file formatting, data-security, verification, and return handling.
  • Supports the annual ACH risk assessment (security of protected information, exposure/return monitoring).
  • Maintains its data-security program (see GLBA WISP) and SOC 2 readiness as part of the ODFI's third-party due diligence.
  • Payvyn TPSP self-assessment: completed at least annually by [owner], confirming the controls above remain in place.

4What the ODFI / TPS must still do

  • Execute the ODFI / Third-Party Sender Origination Agreement and register BizFormCorp as a TPS with Nacha.
  • Complete its own annual Rules Compliance Audit & ACH risk assessment.
  • Set and monitor exposure limits; monitor return rates; handle warranties and indemnities.
