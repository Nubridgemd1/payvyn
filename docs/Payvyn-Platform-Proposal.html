1Executive Summary

Payvyn is a native digital onboarding and payments platform, built and owned by BizFormCorp, that enables the credit union to identify customers, verify their identities, securely link external bank accounts, obtain payment authorization, and move funds into credit-union accounts by ACH or FedNow.

The platform is already built and operational. We propose to configure and integrate Payvyn with the credit union's core so it can power digital customer acquisition, account funding, and additional digital services over time. Because Payvyn is a single, extensible foundation — not a set of siloed tools, and not dependent on any third-party account-linking aggregator — new services can be added as the credit union grows without rebuilding the underlying infrastructure.

Built, deployed & demonstrable today. Payvyn is live as a secure, installable web app at payvyn.org — member onboarding, native bank linking across 4,100+ U.S. institutions, the ACH/FedNow funding flow, and the operations console. Its native bank-ownership verification is already deployed as a live backend (micro-deposits over the credit union's own ACH, no third party) and verified end to end with server-side, attempt-limited security.

2Platform Capabilities

Payvyn delivers four integrated capabilities, all developed natively:

2.1Customer Identification and Verification

  • Digitally capture customer information, with automatic address population.
  • Verify customer identity for compliant CIP/KYC onboarding, including document and selfie capture and OFAC/sanctions screening.
  • Create and retain the compliance record that supports onboarding requirements.

2.2Native External Bank Account Linking

  • Link any of 4,100+ U.S. banks and credit unions through Payvyn's built-in institution directory — no third-party aggregator.
  • Verify the account natively: ABA routing-number validation plus instant or micro-deposit ownership verification.
  • Capture and timestamp the required customer consent and authorization before any debit.

2.3Electronic Account Funding

  • Initiate authorized transfers from the linked external account into the credit-union account.
  • ACH debit for authorized withdrawals, with return handling and pre-funding risk screening.
  • FedNow for eligible instant settlement where the originating and receiving institutions participate.

2.4Integrated Core Operations

  • Connect to the credit union's core banking system through its existing, supported integration layer — alongside the core, with no rip-and-replace.
  • Account crediting, transaction tracking, reconciliation, fraud monitoring, and operational reporting (the Payvyn operations console).
  • An extensible architecture for additional digital financial products.
Easy, low-risk core integration — by design. Payvyn connects to the credit union's core through standards-based interfaces (the core vendor's APIs/connector program, NACHA ACH files, and ISO 20022 messaging), so it layers onto the existing core rather than replacing it. It is compatible with the major core platforms (for example Jack Henry Symitar/Episys, Corelation KeyStone, Fiserv, and FIS), uses vendor-certified connectors where available, and is validated in a sandbox / UAT environment before a phased go-live — keeping the credit union's IT lift and operational risk to a minimum.

3Important Payment Infrastructure Consideration

FedNow is an instant payment service, but it does not itself link external bank accounts or authorize the credit union to debit them.

Starting point: For customers who want to authorize the credit union to pull money from an external bank account, ACH debit is generally the appropriate mechanism. FedNow can complement this by supporting eligible instant payments initiated through participating institutions. Its Request for Payment feature can facilitate payment requests, but the request itself does not move funds — the payment must still be authorized and initiated appropriately.

Accordingly, Payvyn leads with ACH debit for authorized account funding and layers in FedNow instant settlement where participation and arrangements allow — giving the credit union a compliant foundation today and an upgrade path to real-time payments.

4Deployment & Integration Approach

Because Payvyn is already built, deployment is a configuration-and-integration effort rather than a ground-up build. It is phased so the credit union gains value early and each layer is validated before the next is added.

1Identity
Configure onboarding & identity verification. Stand up digital information capture, identity verification, and the compliance (CIP/KYC) data flows for new-customer onboarding.
2Linking
Enable native bank linking & consent. Turn on Payvyn's built-in institution directory and ABA/ownership verification, with auditable consent & authorization capture.
3Funding
Activate ACH debit account funding. Authorized ACH debits from linked external accounts into credit-union accounts, with return handling and risk screening.
4Core
Integrate with the core. Connect through the core's supported APIs/connectors (vendor-certified where available), validated in sandbox/UAT, then phased into production — delivering account crediting, reconciliation, fraud monitoring, and reporting with minimal IT lift and no core replacement.
5Instant & expand
FedNow & new products. Add eligible instant settlement via FedNow, then extend the shared platform to additional digital products and services as the credit union grows.

6Security, Compliance & Governance

Security & data

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • No bank logins or stored banking credentials — accounts are linked by routing/account with ownership verification
  • Least-privilege access & audit logging
  • GLBA Safeguards-aligned controls

Compliance

  • CIP / KYC and BSA/AML + OFAC screening
  • Consent & authorization records for each transfer
  • NACHA rules for ACH; Regulation E protections
  • Reconciliation & reporting for examinations
Governance & roles. The credit union remains the regulated financial institution and system of record — it holds the accounts and owns the BSA/AML program and regulatory relationships. BizFormCorp is the developer and operator of the Payvyn platform, delivering and supporting it under the credit union's oversight.
