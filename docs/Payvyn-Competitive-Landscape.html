Where Payvyn is different: nearly all of the field depends on an aggregator (Plaid/MX) and charges per connection. Payvyn verifies ownership natively, over the credit union's own ACH — no aggregator, no per-link fee, CU-owned. Only a handful operate in this quadrant. The trade-off is same-day, not sub-second, so Payvyn competes on cost & independence, not raw throughput.
