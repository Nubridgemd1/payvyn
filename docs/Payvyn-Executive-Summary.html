Executive SummaryDigital Onboarding & Payments for Credit Unions · Oct 2026
Payvyn is a native digital onboarding and payments platform, built by BizFormCorp, that lets a credit union acquire a member online, verify their identity, link and verify any U.S. bank account, capture authorization, and move funds into the member's account — by ACH or FedNow. It is a single, extensible foundation — not siloed tools, and not dependent on any third-party account-linking aggregator. The platform and its native bank-ownership verification are built and deployed today.

What Payvyn does

1 · Identity & VerificationCapture member information and verify identity for compliant CIP/KYC onboarding, with automatic address population.
2 · Native Bank Linking + OwnershipLink any of 4,100+ U.S. institutions; verify account ownership natively via micro-deposits over the CU's own ACH — no aggregator.
3 · Account FundingAuthorized ACH debit into the member's credit-union account, with FedNow for eligible instant settlement.
4 · Core-Integrated OperationsAccount crediting, transaction tracking, reconciliation, fraud monitoring, and reporting through the core's connectors.

Value to the credit union

  • Digital member acquisition — onboard & fund new members fully online.
  • Deposit & funding growth — frictionless external-to-CU account funding.
  • New fee & transfer revenue — a launchpad for additional products.
  • No aggregator dependency or per-link fees — Payvyn owns the stack.
  • Fast, low-risk deployment — layers onto the existing core, no rip-and-replace.
  • The CU stays in control — regulated institution & system of record.

What's live today

● Platform — a secure, installable web app at https://payvyn.org: member onboarding, native bank linking across 4,100+ U.S. institutions, the ACH/FedNow funding flow, and the Core operations console (tracking, reconciliation, fraud, reporting).

● Native ownership verification — deployed & tested — a live backend that verifies external-account ownership by micro-deposits over the credit union's own ACH (no third party), with server-side, attempt-limited security. Running in sandbox and verified end to end.

Readiness for integration

Payvyn is ready to integrate. The member-facing layer (identity capture, bank linking, ownership verification) and the verification backend are built and deployed. Going live for real money movement is a configuration-and-integration effort — connecting the credit union's ACH origination and core — not a ground-up build. Where identity, bank-linking, and funding are shown as simulated in the live demo, each is swapped for the real rail during integration; the native ownership-verification rail is already real and deployed.

What the credit union provides to go live

  • ACH origination (ODFI) + Same-Day ACH
  • Company ID / routing / settlement account
  • Core-system integration access (Symitar/Episys, KeyStone, Fiserv, FIS)
  • BSA/AML program ownership + Regulation E
  • Identity-verification method (the CU's CIP or a provider)
  • Examiner notification / NCUA non-objection, per policy
