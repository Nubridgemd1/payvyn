Payvyn
Platform ArchitectureA native onboarding, verification & pay-in layer any credit union or fintech can adopt
Payvyn is a drop-in layer that sits in front of your institution. It handles member onboarding, identity & bank verification, and the pay-in — then hands verified members and funded transactions to your systems. It rides your own rails (your ACH or processor, your CIP, your core or ledger) with no aggregator, and it is modular: adopt the pieces you need.
Payvyn does not access your core — and never holds funds.
Payvyn plugs in in front of your institution. It never connects to, reads from, or writes to your core or ledger, and it is non-custodial — funds settle only to your (or your sponsor bank's) account. Every interaction is a hand-off: Payvyn passes you verified members and ready-to-process files/records; you remain the system of record and the custodian.

5Adoption models — start least-privilege

A. File-based RECOMMENDED START

  • Zero core access. Secure file exchange (SFTP): applicant → CIP; NACHA → ACH; a posting/settlement file → you post.
  • You stay the only party that writes to the core.
  • Fastest to approve — minimal security review, no API project.

B. API-integrated PHASE 2

  • Direct, real-time calls to your core/ledger & processor APIs.
  • Scoped client limited to named operations — never open or standing access.
  • Smoother member experience (instant open + post).

6Security & posture

  • No core access — Payvyn hands off; you remain the system of record and the only writer to your core.
  • Non-custodial — funds settle only to your (or your sponsor bank's) account; Payvyn never holds funds.
  • Scoped credentials per integration (CIP, ACH/processor, optional core API) — no standing admin access.
  • Data protection — SSN/account numbers encrypted in transit & at rest; full numbers stay server-side, never in the browser.
  • Native & free baseline — SSN validity + OFAC screening run at $0, before anything reaches your paid systems.
  • Audit — every hand-off, origination, and return is logged and reconciled.
