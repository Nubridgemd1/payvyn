PayExpress Pay-In ArchitectureHow Payvyn verifies the sender's bank & pulls funds over HHFCU's ACH · for Heritage Hub FCU
In the PayExpress program, Payvyn is the U.S. pay-in rail: it verifies the sender's external bank account and prepares the ACH to pull the funds. HHFCU originates that ACH as the ODFI and holds the settlement (FBO) — HHFCU is the only party on the ACH network and the only party that holds funds. Payvyn generates the standard NACHA files; HHFCU originates them.
No access to your core — only hand-offs.
Payvyn never connects to, logs into, reads, or writes to HHFCU's core, online banking, or member data, and it never originates ACH or holds funds. Every interaction is a hand-off: Payvyn sends you a ready-to-originate NACHA file, and you send back the standard ACH returns — nothing more, and never a live connection into your systems. HHFCU is the ODFI (the only party on the ACH rail) and holds all funds in its FBO/settlement account. Payvyn is non-custodial.

1Architecture at a glance

Payvyn prepares; HHFCU originates. Only HHFCU touches the ACH network and holds the funds.

2The two ACH operations Payvyn uses

A. Account-ownership verification

  • Payvyn generates a NACHA file of two small micro-deposit credits to the sender's account.
  • HHFCU originates them (Same-Day ACH); SEC PPD.
  • The sender confirms the two amounts in the app → ownership verified (3 attempts, then locked).
  • If HHFCU offers instant verification, that can replace micro-deposits.

B. Funding debit

  • On the sender's authorization, Payvyn generates a NACHA debit for the send amount.
  • HHFCU originates it; SEC WEB (internet-initiated consumer debit).
  • Funds settle to HHFCU's FBO account (USD) — the Transmitter of Record.
  • From there, PayExpress proceeds to the payout leg (IMTO).

3Pay-in flow, step by step

  1. Bank entry — the sender enters routing + account; Payvyn validates the ABA routing checksum and recognizes the institution (no login, no aggregator).
  2. Verification file — Payvyn queues two micro-deposits and generates the NACHA file; HHFCU originates it over Same-Day ACH.
  3. Confirm — the two deposits land in the sender's bank; the sender enters the amounts in the app → ownership verified.
  4. Authorize — the sender authorizes the transfer amount (consent captured & timestamped).
  5. Funding file — Payvyn generates the NACHA debit; HHFCU originates it; funds settle to the FBO account.
  6. Returns — HHFCU delivers any ACH returns (R-codes) back to Payvyn, which fails/reverses the transfer cleanly.
  7. Reconcile — Payvyn matches settlement + returns and confirms the pay-in; PayExpress then proceeds to the payout leg.

4Who does what

Payvyn (prepares)

  • Collect + validate the sender's routing/account (ABA checksum).
  • Run ownership verification (micro-deposit logic, attempt-limiting, fraud controls).
  • Generate the NACHA files (credits + debit).
  • Track status, process returns, reconcile.
  • Encrypt account data; keep full numbers server-side only.
  • Never originates ACH, holds funds, or accesses HHFCU's core.

HHFCU (originates & holds)

  • Originates the NACHA files over its ACH as the ODFI — the only party on the ACH network.
  • Holds all funds in its FBO/settlement account.
  • Delivers ACH returns (R-codes) back to Payvyn.
  • Owns the BSA/AML program & is the Transmitter of Record.
  • Sets origination limits & cutoffs.

What we need back from you: only the standard ACH return file — R-codes such as R01 (insufficient funds), R03 (no account), R04 (invalid account). Payvyn matches each return by trace number + our reference (Individual ID) and fails or reverses the transfer automatically. That return file is the entire loop — no portal, no core access, no manual lookups on your side.
